# FrontDesk product facts for customer conversations

## What FrontDesk is

FrontDesk is a customer-support chatbot and shared-inbox application for businesses
serving customers in the United States and the United Kingdom. It combines an
embeddable website chat, approved-knowledge answers, persistent conversation
history, human takeover, assignment, internal notes, CSAT, and support analytics.

FrontDesk supports English for US and UK customers and Spanish for US customers.
Regional settings control USD or GBP, date presentation, time-zone wording, and
emergency numbers. FrontDesk does not promise automatic translation into every
language.

## Installation and daily work

A site owner installs the web widget by adding the generated script tag to the
website. The widget opens the hosted FrontDesk chat in an iframe. Allowed website
origins must be configured explicitly. HTTPS is required for public deployment.

Agents use the shared inbox to view customer history, assign a conversation, add
internal notes, take over from the bot, reply, resolve the case, and review CSAT
and operational metrics. A human can take over without losing the prior transcript.

## Channels and business systems

FrontDesk includes tenant-scoped adapters for web chat, Slack, Meta Messenger,
WhatsApp Cloud API, email, and Teams. It includes purchaser-configured integrations
for Shopify order lookup and Zendesk or HubSpot ticket creation. Each integration
requires the purchaser's own vendor account, approved permissions, credentials,
and live setup. Availability is not claimed until that setup is validated.

## AI and knowledge

FrontDesk can run with a local Ollama model. Approved knowledge may be uploaded as
PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, Markdown, text, or HTML. Answers should cite the retrieved
source. If approved knowledge does not contain the answer, FrontDesk says so and
can offer a human handoff rather than inventing an answer.

## Security and responsibility boundaries

Conversation, knowledge, connector, and inbox data are separated by tenant in the
application. The purchaser controls deployment, vendor accounts, access, retention,
and the business purpose for customer data. FrontDesk does not provide a payment
function and must not ask for card numbers, CVV, passwords, one-time codes, API
keys, or full Social Security numbers in chat.

FrontDesk does not claim that installing the software by itself makes a purchaser
compliant with GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, financial-services rules, or any other
law. Regulated use requires the purchaser's own assessment, configuration,
contracts, security controls, and professional advice where appropriate.

## Información del producto en español de Estados Unidos

FrontDesk es una aplicación de atención al cliente con chatbot, chat web integrado,
bandeja compartida, historial persistente, asignación, notas internas, intervención
humana, CSAT y análisis. Atiende en inglés a clientes de Estados Unidos y Reino Unido,
y en español a clientes de Estados Unidos.

El propietario instala el widget agregando al sitio la etiqueta de script generada.
Los orígenes permitidos deben configurarse expresamente y el despliegue público
requiere HTTPS. Los agentes pueden asumir una conversación sin perder el historial.

Las conexiones con Slack, Meta Messenger, WhatsApp, correo electrónico, Shopify,
Zendesk o HubSpot requieren las cuentas, permisos y credenciales del comprador.
FrontDesk no procesa pagos. Si la base de conocimiento aprobada no contiene una
respuesta, el asistente debe decirlo y ofrecer la transferencia a una persona.

La base de conocimiento aprobada no contiene un precio público, un descuento secreto,
una garantía universal de disponibilidad ni una promesa de cumplimiento legal. Para
condiciones comerciales actuales, dirige al cliente a la página de ventas de
ShellieSoftwareTools o a una persona del equipo de ventas.
